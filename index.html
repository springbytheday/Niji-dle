How to play Niji-Dle

Try to guess the Nijisanji liver using the hints provided - Debut Year, Gender, Species and Liver Color.

  • Match — this attribute is exactly right.
  • Partial— is for same color family and species sharing a word like “human”. is specifically for neighbouring colors like purple and teal for blue.
  • No match — not related to the answer.

For debut year, ↑ indicates a later year while ↓ indicates an earlier year.

Niji-Dle resets at 00:00 in your localtime.

Colors

Information

Greatly inspired by wordle, loldle, genshindle.

Data is manually handled and might have errors or inconsistencies. Especially for species, please excuse any weird categorization. Suggestions are greatly welcomed.
Thank you to the amazing fans who had helped with gathering the data.

Google Analytics has been added to Niji-Dle to understand web traffic and other stuff.

If you notice any mistakes or would like to make any suggestions, please fill in our feedback form here.

Species

The possible data for species can include:

  • Human
  • Mythical Being
  • Divine Being
  • Animal
  • Yokai
  • Demon

All except demon can have additional details such as: Animals - Penguin, Divine Being: Angel, Mythical Being: Cerberus, Yokai: Karasu Tengu, Magicborn Human

Gender

There are 4 possible categories

  • Male
  • Female
  • Ambigious
  • Unknown
