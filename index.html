How to play Niji-Dle

Try to guess today's Nijisanji liver using the hints provided.

  • Match — this attribute is exactly right.
  • Partial — close, but not exact (e.g. same color family, or species sharing a word like “human”).
  • No match — not related to the answer.
  • Unknown — that detail isn’t available for this liver, so it’s never counted right or wrong.

For debut year, ↑ indicates a later year while ↓ indicates an earlier year.
